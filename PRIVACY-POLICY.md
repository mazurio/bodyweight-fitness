# Bodyweight Fitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy describes how the Bodyweight Fitness app for iOS, including its optional Platinium subscription, handles information. The app is provided by Damian Mazurkiewicz. For privacy questions or requests, contact **damian@mazur.io**.

## Information stored on your device

You can use the app without creating an account or providing your name. The app stores information you enter or generate while using it, including:

- Your optional display name and app preferences.
- Workout history, exercises, repetitions, weights, hold times and workout dates.
- Custom exercises, routines, programmes, schedules and reminder settings.
- Progress information such as personal records, XP, levels and badges.

The app uses this information to provide workout tracking, progress summaries, training suggestions and other features you choose to use. These calculations take place on your device. The app does not send your name or training history to a developer-operated server. It does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and your training data is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

## Backups and files you choose to share

If you use manual backup or export, the app creates a file containing training data and preferences. You choose where to save or share it using the system file picker or sharing controls. Choosing iCloud Drive or another storage provider sends the file to that provider under its own privacy policy and your account settings.

Exported backup files are not encrypted by the app. Anyone with access to a file may be able to read its contents. Keep these files in a location you trust.

Restoring a backup replaces the app's current training data and creates a local recovery copy of the previous data. The app does not provide automatic training-data synchronisation between devices. Separately, Apple's device backup services may include app data depending on your device and backup settings.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases, payments, renewals and refunds. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to check purchase and subscription status, unlock Platinium features and restore purchases. It processes transaction information, such as the purchased product, expiry and revocation status, for these purposes. The developer does not receive your payment-card details from the app.

Apple handles information associated with your Apple Account and App Store use under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Reminders

If you enable workout reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. You can change reminders in the app and withdraw notification permission in iOS Settings. Reminders do not require a developer-operated push-notification server.

## Email support and external links

If you send feedback or contact support, the developer receives your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve support issues. The app does not automatically attach your training database to feedback emails. Your email provider also processes the message under its own terms.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, related follow-up and any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing damian@mazur.io.

When you open an external link, such as YouTube, GitHub, this policy or Apple's terms, that service may receive information such as your IP address and browser or device information. Its own privacy policy applies to your visit. Your training history is not attached to these links.

## Retention, deletion and your choices

Local training information remains on your device until you delete it through available app controls or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app may retain its data. Exported files, recovery exports and device backups are separate copies; deleting an entry or deleting the app does not remove copies stored elsewhere. Manage those copies through Files, your storage provider or your device backup settings.

The developer cannot access, retrieve or remotely erase training data stored only on your device or in your own backups. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information held by the developer, restrict or object to its processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact damian@mazur.io to exercise those rights. For support correspondence, information is processed to respond to your request and for the legitimate interest of providing support; legal obligations may require limited retention.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or information-handling practices change. Updates will be published at this address with a revised date. Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.
